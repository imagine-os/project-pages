HoyOS Navigation Architecture
HoyOS · UX architecture review

Fewer doors, clearer rooms

An audit of how HoyOS is organised today, across the customer app, the teacher app and the staff workspace, and a proposal to restructure navigation around the jobs each person does at the studio, five days before opening.

Audited 25 Oct 2026, live build 0c2c2feaScope routes, menus, role homes, entry pointsNot in scope visual style (Pulso Luxe stays)

What’s there today

130routes across 23 modules
11roles, 4 shells (customer, teacher, staff, build)
28menu items an admin sees, in 6 groups
37for a super admin, design tools included
22flat links on the customer “Más” page

The pages themselves are in good shape after the Pulso rounds. The problem is the structure around them: the menus grew one module at a time, so they mirror how the code is organised rather than how the studio works. Each new feature added a menu item, and nothing ever merged.

Principles for the new structure

  1. Organise by the job, not the module.Groups answer “what am I doing right now?”: running today, talking to people, selling, money, the studio itself.
  2. One home per person.Every role lands on one page built for their day. “Panel” and “Inicio” become one role-aware Inicio.
  3. Merge before you add.Related pages become tabs of one item (Caja: cobrar · pendientes; Mensajes: WhatsApp · correo). A new feature joins an existing room before it gets a door.
  4. Product and build stay apart.Design and dev tools move to their own Build workspace, reached by a switcher only super admins and developers see. Status like “beta” becomes a small badge, never part of a label.
  5. Seven items or fewer for most people.Front desk, finance and marketing should see a menu they can hold in their head. Admin gets more, still grouped in fours.

Plan

Phase 11–2 days · menu config only

Re-group without touching pages Before 30 Oct

  • New groups and labels through each route’s nav.group / nav.order; no page changes.
  • Status as badges: strip demo/prototipo/borradores/prueba from labels; add a Beta badge to the nav item.
  • Build switcher: design and dev routes move to their own sidebar for super admin and developer.
  • Fix the doors: avatar → Perfil, wordmark → Inicio, page codes only in dev mode, remove the gift trial from the dock.
Phase 23–4 days · tabbed merges

Merge related pages into one room

  • Mensajes: one inbox with WhatsApp and correo tabs; templates move to Marketing.
  • Caja: cobrar + pendientes. Miembros: CRM + eliminaciones. Nómina: + pago por clase. Gastos: + apps. Salas: + mantenimiento.
  • Ventas: Planes y productos, Bonos y regalos (incl. en recepción), Promociones.
  • Old URLs redirect to their tab; a shared PageTabs pattern goes into D-02.
Phase 32–3 days · customer app

Four-room dock and a grouped Perfil

  • Mis clases becomes a hub: próximas, paquete, historial y práctica.
  • Perfil groups the 22 links into membresía, estudio, comparte, cuenta.
  • Inicio quick actions trimmed to three.
Phase 42–3 days · cross-cutting

Role homes and polish

  • One role-aware Inicio per staff role (front desk, coordinator, finance, marketing, maintenance, admin).
  • Command bar (Ctrl K) over pages, members and classes, using the existing global search.
  • Fix the no-access flash; one page-header pattern (title, tabs, primary action) across the workspace.

Decisions needed

Do we do Phase 1 before opening day?It’s menu configuration only, low risk, and it removes the build labels members and staff would otherwise see on 30 Oct.
Does front desk see Miembros (CRM)?Today they see CRM and WhatsApp. The proposal keeps both, as Miembros and Mensajes.
Does the gift trial stay visible to members at launch?Proposed: yes, as a Home card, but not in the dock.
NamesMensajes vs Bandeja, Caja vs Registrar y cobrar, Inicio for every role. These need Jas and Justin, like the status vocabulary.
